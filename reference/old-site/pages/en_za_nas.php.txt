ART AQUA is a brand of the company PostModerna, and is one of the first companies in Macedonia specialized in designer bathroom equipment (import, trade and installation/reconstruction ). Since 1997, this company has successfully operated on the Macedonian market and is now recognizable brand that offers high quality services. Our exceptional product range results from our commitment to introduce into the Macedonian market the latest European designs and trends, while ensuring the best quality and functionality to our customers. Our wide range of products includes sanitary ware, bath tubs, shower trays, hydro massage systems, shower enclosures, bathroom furniture, taps, showers and bathroom accessories. We are suppliers of renowned Italian brands (CERAMICA GRAVENA, GSG, HATRIA, FRIGES, REMER, FIORE, EFFEPI etc.) Slovenian brands (KOLPA, REFLEX, ARMAL, UNITAS HERC, MINES etc.) and the well-known Croatian AQUAESTIL. The key to the ongoing ART AQUA brand success is the professional and experienced staff, who have excellent skills and understanding of the technical characteristics of the products. We understand the needs of our clients and will endeavor to find the best solution for your project, thus making the experience with ART AQUA memorable. Your bathroom is more then just a place for maintaining personal hygiene. It is also a source of health and a reflection of your home.
